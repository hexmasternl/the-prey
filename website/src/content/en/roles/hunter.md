## Your Goal

You are the Hunter, the only one in the game. Catch every Prey before the game timer reaches zero. If you do, the Hunter wins.

---

## Head Start: Hold Your Position

When the game starts the screen tells you: **Stay in this exact position.** You cannot chase or tag yet. Moving more than 25 meters from where you started before the countdown ends gives you a **10-minute penalty**.

Prey positions already appear on your map, so use the wait to pick your first target and plan your route.

---

## Your HUD

{{< screenshot name="hunter-hud" alt="The Hunter HUD: the Hunter and Live pills at the top, the Tag button and four tiles at the bottom" caption="The Hunter HUD" >}}

- **Role pill** (top left): red **Hunter**.
- **Game clock** (top right): **Live** and the time left. Green with more than 10 minutes left, orange from 10 minutes, red from 5. During a penalty it turns red and reads **Penalty**.
- **Next ping**: seconds until the next Prey positions arrive.
- **Preys in play**: Prey still in the game, out of the total.
- **Closest prey**: the distance to the nearest Prey's last known location, not their live position.
- **Penalised preys**: how many Prey have a penalty right now. A penalised Prey's position comes in about every 30 seconds.

Prey markers on your map sit at the position of their last ping. They have probably moved since. Close the gap fast.

## Tagging a Prey

{{< screenshot name="tag-sheet" alt="The Tag a prey sheet listing the Prey within 50 meters with their distance" caption="Preys within tagging range" >}}

The red **Tag** button sits above the tiles. It stays locked until the head start is over. Tap it to see every Prey within **50 meters** of you, with their distance. The server decides who is in range, so a Prey further away cannot be tagged. Pick one and tap **Tag prey**. If the Prey got away just before, the app tells you and refreshes the list.

The tag takes effect straight away. The tagged Prey becomes a spectator.

---

## Working the Field

You hunt alone, so plan your route. The map shows every Prey at once. Go for the ones near the edge of the playfield, where escape routes are fewest, and watch the Next ping countdown: the moment new positions arrive is the moment to move.
