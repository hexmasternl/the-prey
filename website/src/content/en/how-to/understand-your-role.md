description: "Understand your role mid-game in The Prey: the safety warning, the head start, what the Prey and Hunter HUDs show, tagging, and the result at the end."

---

1. When the game screen opens, confirm the safety warning: **I will be careful**. Keep your eyes up and watch for traffic.
2. The head start countdown appears. As **Prey**: run, the Hunter already sees your pings. As **Hunter**: stay in this exact position. Moving more than 25 meters gives you a 10-minute penalty.
3. The first time you play a role, a short tour points out every part of your HUD. You can skip it.
4. As **Prey**, watch the top-right pill: **Safe**, **Close**, **Warning** or **Unknown** tells you how near the Hunter is. The **Next broadcast** countdown shows when your position goes to the Hunter again. Move before it reaches zero.
5. As **Hunter**, watch **Next ping**: new Prey positions arrive when it hits zero. **Closest prey** is the distance to the nearest last known location, not a live position.
6. Every marker on the map is a last known location. Assume the player has moved since.
7. As **Hunter**, tap **Tag** once the head start is over. The list shows the Prey within 50 meters. Pick one and tap **Tag prey**.
8. Watch the time. In the final stage pings come faster, so cover gets harder to keep. Prey, stay hidden and wait it out. Hunter, close in fast.
9. Tagged? You stay in the game as a spectator until it ends.
10. When the game ends, the result appears for your role. Tap **Back to menu**.
