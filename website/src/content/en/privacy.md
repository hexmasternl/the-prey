The Prey is a GPS-based hide-and-seek game. Because the game tracks player locations in real time, we take your privacy seriously. This statement explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and the rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

---

## Who We Are

The Prey is operated from the Netherlands and reachable at **theprey.nl**. For any privacy-related question or request, contact us at **privacy@theprey.nl**.

## What Data We Collect

| Category | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Name or nickname, email address, authentication identifier | To create and secure your account |
| Location data | GPS coordinates while a game is active | Core gameplay: tracking Prey and the Hunter |
| Approximate position (Near me) | Your position rounded to about 1 km, sent once when you use Near me | To list public playfields near you; not stored |

## Location Data

Location is the heart of The Prey, so it deserves special mention:

- GPS coordinates are collected **only while you are taking part in an active game**.
- Locations are shared with other players strictly according to the game rules (for example, Prey positions are broadcast to the Hunter throughout the game).
- When a game ends, live location sharing stops, and every GPS location collected during the game is permanently deleted from our servers within 48 hours.
- We do not track your location outside of an active game, in the foreground or in the background.
- When you use **Near me** on the public playfields, the app sends your **approximate position** (rounded to about 1 km) **once**, to find playfields nearby. It is used only to answer that search: it is **not stored**, logged or shared.

## In-Game Location Tracking (Android App)

Before the app asks for location permission, it shows a notice that explains how location tracking works. In short:

- The Prey collects your device's **precise location** and sends it to our servers **only while you are in an active game**.
- Collection continues while the app is closed or not in use, in the background, or while the screen is off, so the game keeps working when your phone is in your pocket.
- Tracking runs as a **foreground service**, with a **persistent notification** for as long as it runs.
- Tracking **stops as soon as the game ends or you leave it**.
- Your position is used to show players to the Hunter and to check that you stay inside the playfield. It is only shared with the players of that game.
- When you use **Near me** on the public playfields, the app sends your approximate position once to find nearby playfields. It is not stored.

Outside a game, the main menu only shows your current coordinates on your own screen; they are not sent to our servers. The only exception is Near me, and only when you tap it: then a position rounded to about 1 km is sent once and not stored.

The main menu shows this notice by itself only once on your phone. You can choose **Not now** and still use the menus. Starting or joining a game requires precise location, so the app shows the notice again at that point, and you can grant location access later on the app's Settings screen, which shows the same notice. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings. On Android 13 and later the app also asks for permission to show notifications, so the tracking notification can appear.

## Why We Process Your Data

We process your data to:

- Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
- Run games, including real-time location tracking and notifications.
- Store the playfields and game history needed to replay and improve the experience.
- Maintain the security, stability, and performance of the service.

Our legal bases are the **performance of a contract** (providing the game you asked to play) and our **legitimate interest** in operating a secure, reliable service.

---

## Who We Share Data With

We share data only with service providers that help us run The Prey:

- **Authentication**: our identity provider (Auth0) manages secure sign-in.
- **Cloud hosting**: our backend runs on Microsoft Azure within the European Union.
- **Other players**: during a game, limited gameplay data (such as your role and, where the rules allow, your location) is visible to other participants.

These providers process data on our behalf under agreements that require appropriate safeguards.

---

## How Long We Keep Data

- **Account data** is kept for as long as your account exists.
- **Game data**, including each game's player information, roles, tags, results, and every GPS location collected during play, is **permanently deleted from our servers 48 hours after the game finishes**.
- **Diagnostic logs** are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

When you delete your account, we remove or anonymise your personal data, except where we are legally required to retain it.

## Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
- Restrict or object to certain processing.
- Receive your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email **privacy@theprey.nl**. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
