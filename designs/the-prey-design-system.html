What changed in v2.0
Same palette and fonts. Every hex value and both typefaces carry over unchanged.
Tokens first. Named primitive and semantic tokens, a 4pt scale, a radius scale (4–20px) and three elevation levels replace one-off values.
Calmer and more legible. No scanline overlay, glow kept for live and pressed states, 48px touch targets, labels moved from Ghost to Soft so they pass contrast.
01 · Brand

Logo

The new mark is a fox in the crosshairs. The broken reticle is the hunter's view and the faceted fox mask is the prey. A green lock dot sits between its eyes. Everything is built from flat geometry, so the mark stays sharp at 16px and holds up as a single colour.

Construction & clear space

Rules
  • · 512 grid. Reticle radius 168, stroke 18, round caps, gaps of 28° at each cardinal.
  • · The fox mask is symmetric about the vertical axis. Its right half takes a 20% Void overlay to give it a facet.
  • · Clear space on all sides equals the length of one reticle tick (1/8 of the mark width).
  • · Minimum size: 24px for the mark, 16px for the app icon (favicon).
  • · Only ever on Void, Base or Surface. When a light background can't be avoided, use the app icon tile.
  • · One colour version: Signal on dark, or Void on Signal. Never red alone.

Do & don't

Use the icon tile or the mark on dark surfaces.
Don't put the full-colour mark on light backgrounds.
Don't stretch, skew or crop the mark.
Don't recolour it or add glows and effects.
02 · Brand

Principles

The Prey runs on a phone but it's played in the street. The interface is a surveillance instrument for a manhunt: night-vision optics, GPS trackers and field-radio comms. Phosphor green burning against dead grey.

01

Glanceable over beautiful

Critical state (your role, the nearest threat, the time left) has to register in under a second. Hierarchy beats decoration.

02

Green is information

Signal green is the most precious pixel on screen. Keep it for live, actionable data. If everything glows, nothing does.

03

Two sides, one system

Prey and Hunter share one instrument. Role shows through a single accent (green or red), never through a different layout.

04

The world is the canvas

Map and radar come first. Chrome stays thin and translucent and recedes so the playfield dominates.

03 · Brand

Voice & tone

Short, imperative, radio-operator calm. Say what is happening, then what to do. No exclamation marks, emoji or apologies.

Do
  • You are hidden. Keep moving.
  • Hunter nearby. Break line of sight.
  • Signal lost. Find open sky.
  • Hunt begins in 00:10.
Don't
  • Oops! We couldn't find you 😅
  • You might want to consider moving.
  • Error 403: location unavailable.
  • Welcome back, valued player!

05 · Foundations

Typography

Two faces with two jobs. Special Elite is a battered typewriter face that stamps headings and big readouts like a printed dossier. PT Mono handles everything else, and its fixed width keeps live numbers from jittering.

Special Elite has one weight. Create emphasis with size and colour, never faux-bold. Labels are uppercase PT Mono with +1.5px tracking, in Soft rather than Ghost. Keep body lines under ~68 characters.

08 · Foundations

Iconography

A 24px grid with 2px strokes, round caps and no fills except for focal dots. Icons take the colour of their text. Pair them with a label wherever space allows.

15 · Components

Sheets & dialogs

On mobile, prefer bottom sheets: they're reachable with one thumb and keep the map visible. Use centred dialogs only to confirm something destructive. The scrim is Void at 70%.

19 · Game UI

Radar & map

The radar is the signature element: Void disc, Signal Deep rings every 25% of radius, a linear 3.4s sweep. You are the light dot in the centre. The map uses a dark, desaturated style so markers are the brightest things on screen.

21 · Build

Tokens

Use the semantic tokens in components. Primitives exist only to define them. Copy these into the app's global stylesheet, or map them onto Ionic's --ion-* variables.

/* primitives */
--tp-void: #0C0E0C;  --tp-base: #181B17;  --tp-surface: #23271F;  --tp-surface-2: #2D3328;  --tp-line: #39402F;
--tp-signal: #64FF00;  --tp-signal-dim: #3F9E00;  --tp-signal-deep: #1F4D00;
--tp-hunter: #FF2F1F;  --tp-caution: #FFB300;  --tp-info: #13E0C8;
--tp-text: #DCF6D2;  --tp-text-soft: #8C9A83;  --tp-text-ghost: #5A6553;

/* semantic */
--bg: var(--tp-base);  --bg-inset: var(--tp-void);  --bg-card: var(--tp-surface);  --bg-raised: var(--tp-surface-2);
--fg: var(--tp-text);  --fg-muted: var(--tp-text-soft);  --fg-disabled: var(--tp-text-ghost);  --border: var(--tp-line);
--accent: var(--tp-signal);  --on-accent: var(--tp-void);  --danger: var(--tp-hunter);  --warning: var(--tp-caution);
--info: var(--tp-info);

/* type */
--font-display: 'Special Elite', 'Courier New', monospace;
--font-body: 'PT Mono', 'Courier New', monospace;

/* space · shape · depth · motion */
--s-1: 4px … --s-16: 64px;   --r-xs: 4px; --r-sm: 8px; --r-md: 12px; --r-lg: 20px; --r-pill: 999px;
