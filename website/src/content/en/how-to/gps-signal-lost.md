---
title: "GPS Signal Lost"
weight: 6
description: "What to do when your location stops in The Prey: check the location permission, battery saver and the tracking notification, and resume tracking before you are ruled out."
keywords: ["GPS signal lost", "location not updating", "Allow all the time", "tracking notification", "GPS troubleshooting", "The Prey help"]
---

1. Act fast. If no location from your phone reaches the server for 5 minutes you become passive, and after 7 minutes you are out. For a Prey, out counts as caught.
2. Check the notification shade. During a game The Prey shows a **Game in progress** notification. If it says **Location unavailable**, the server cannot see you.
3. Open your phone's **Settings → Location** and make sure location is on.
4. Open **Settings → Apps → The Prey → Permissions → Location**. Choose **Allow all the time** and switch on **Use precise location**.
5. Turn off **Battery Saver** during the game. It can stop location updates in the background.
6. Open The Prey. The main menu shows **Resume game** while your game is running. Tap it to return to the game screen.
7. Restarted your phone? Tap the **Your game is still running** notification to resume tracking. Until then the server sees you as silent.
8. Move into the open. Tall buildings, tunnels and dense trees weaken the GPS signal.
9. The app's **Settings → Location** shows your current access: it must read **All the time**.
